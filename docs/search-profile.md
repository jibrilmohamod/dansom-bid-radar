# Dansom opportunity finder: search profile and sources

Draft for Jibril to trim. Built from the Dansom company profile (2024) and decisions made 9 Oct 2026.

## 1. Where to look

| Priority | Countries | How they're weighted |
|---|---|---|
| Highest | **Somalia** (including Somaliland, Puntland, Jubaland, South West, Hirshabelle, Galmudug, Banadir) | Full score |
| Included | **Kenya** (especially the northeast: Garissa, Wajir, Mandera, Dadaab) and **Ethiopia** (especially the Somali Region) | Small score penalty, so they rank below similar Somalia items |
| Included | Regional "Horn of Africa", "East Africa" or "IGAD" assignments covering Somalia | Treated like Somalia |

## 2. What counts as a match

**Strong match (Dansom's core work)**
- Third-party monitoring (TPM), TPME, monitoring and verification, field monitoring
- Evaluations: baseline, midline, endline, final or impact evaluation, programme review, MEAL systems
- Political economy analysis, conflict analysis, peace and conflict assessment, stakeholder mapping
- Security risk assessment and security advisory
- Formative research, surveys, perception surveys, data collection, enumerators

**Good match**
- Policy advisory, institutional diagnostics, public sector or pay reform
- Statistics capacity building
- Environmental and social impact assessment (EIA/ESIA), ESMP and EMMP monitoring
- Organisational capacity building, HR and recruitment services
- Event, logistics and consultant-management services for donor programmes

**Tech (growth area Dansom wants to break into, added 9 Oct 2026)**
- Digital M&E and data platforms, dashboards, MIS, data systems, survey tech (ODK, KoBo, SurveyCTO)
- GIS, remote sensing and satellite monitoring, remote or digital TPM
- Digital transformation, e-government, digital public infrastructure, ICT assessments and strategies
- Software or web development and IT systems for donor programmes or ministries
- Data science, AI and analytics consultancies

Tech items are tagged "Tech" so they can be filtered. They score as a good match, or as a strong match when combined with M&E or research. Dansom's Global Education Policy Dashboard work for the World Bank is the obvious reference project.

**Sectors that add weight:** governance, education, health and GBV, WASH, agriculture and food security, livelihoods, climate resilience, durable solutions and IDPs.

**Filtered out:** goods and works tenders (vehicles, construction, supplies), jobs for individual staff, and award notices. Individual-consultant posts are kept only when Dansom could field the expert as a firm.

## 3. Donors to watch first

These are Dansom's past and current clients: the EU Delegation to Somalia, World Bank and IFC, UNSOS, UNOPS, WFP, FAO, UNDP, SDC (Swiss), DANIDA (Danish), GIZ, FCDO, Save the Children, CARE, NRC, Solidarités and ACTED. A match from one of them scores higher.

Opportunities that suit a **consortium with Particip** (larger EU and World Bank framework contracts) get flagged as such.

## 4. Sources

I tested each site from here on 9 Oct 2026.

| Source | What it has | Can it be read automatically? |
|---|---|---|
| World Bank procurement notices | Expressions of interest and RFPs for consultancy work on World Bank projects | **Yes**, it has a public data feed and Somalia filtering works |
| GIZ tender portal | GIZ tenders and expert pools | **Yes**, the public table can be read. Local GIZ tenders in Somalia and Kenya are handled by country offices |
| UNDP procurement notices | Country-level RFPs and consultancies, including for Somalia, Kenya and Ethiopia | **Partly.** The page I reached showed 2024 items, so UNDP's newer supplier portal needs checking |
| UNGM (all UN agencies) | UN tenders: UNSOS, UNOPS, WFP, FAO, UNICEF and others | **Needs a login.** Nothing is visible without signing in. A free supplier registration may fix this |
| EU Funding & Tenders / TED | EU service contracts and framework calls | **Not from here yet.** The site is a script-driven app, and its data feed refused my test |
| ReliefWeb | NGO consultancies and evaluation ToRs, very strong for Somalia | **Not from here yet.** Its data feed now requires a registered app name |
| SomaliJobs tenders | Local NGO and UN tenders in Somalia | **Not from here yet.** The listings load by script |
| Kenya PPIP and Ethiopia e-GP | Government tenders | Low fit with Dansom's work, so leave these for later |
